BRIDGE connects people with meaningful ways to support refugees and build stronger, more welcoming communities. We're starting in Kansas with a vision that reaches far beyond.

Our Mission

BRIDGE exists to connect people, communities, and organizations so that everyone can find meaningful ways to support refugees and create welcoming communities.


We are beginning in Kansas, but our vision is global. We want to make it easier for anyone, anywhere, to discover how they can help.

How Can You Help?

Everyone has something they can contribute. Find a way to get involved that works for you.

🤝 Volunteer

Give your time to organizations and community projects supporting refugees.

📚 Share Your Skills

Use your skills, knowledge, language abilities, or experience to support your community.

📦 Donate Items

Find organizations that are currently collecting useful items and supplies.

📣 Spread Awareness

Share accurate information and help others discover ways they can contribute.
